StepShot Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

StepShot ("the extension") is a Chrome extension that records your interactions with a web page as before and after screenshots, for testing and bug reporting. This policy explains what the extension does and doesn't do with your information.

What the extension records

Only while you are recording, and only on the tab where you pressed "Record this tab", the extension saves:

  • Screenshots of the page before and after each interaction.
  • What you interacted with: the element's label, its position, and the text you typed. Text typed into password and payment card fields is never recorded.
  • Console messages and errors from the page.
  • Network requests made by the page: addresses, status, timing and headers. Credential headers such as Authorization and Cookie are removed. The response body is kept only for failed API calls.

When you are not recording, the extension records nothing.

Where your data goes

Nowhere. Recordings are stored on your computer, in the extension's own storage in your browser. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. The extension has no server, no account system and no analytics.

When you export a session, the extension saves a file to your computer. What you do with that file is up to you. Exported reports contain everything listed above, so check them before sharing.

What is stored on your device

  • Your recorded sessions, until you delete them in the extension.
  • Your choice for the "capture the full page" option.

All of it is removed if you uninstall the extension.

The waitlist

The "Join the waitlist" button opens a Google Form in a new tab. The extension sends nothing to that form. If you fill it in, Google and we receive what you type there, which we use only to contact you about StepShot Pro.

What we don't do

  • We don't use analytics or tracking.
  • We don't sell, rent or share your data.
  • We don't use your data for advertising or creditworthiness.
